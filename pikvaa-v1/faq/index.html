What is Pikvaa

What is Pikvaa?

Pikvaa is a studio app for Indian photographers that runs the whole job in one place: leads, quotes, bookings, crew, upload, client gallery and photo selection, payment records and final delivery. It is built for solo and small studios of two to five staff, works in Tamil and English, and is designed for low-end Android phones and slow networks.

Who is Pikvaa made for?

Pikvaa is made for small Indian photography studios, from solo photographers to teams of two to five staff, starting with Tamil Nadu. It suits wedding, engagement, pre-wedding, baby, birthday and event photographers who want client selection, payment tracking and delivery in one app instead of juggling Google Drive, WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

What can I do with Pikvaa as a photographer?

With Pikvaa you can capture leads, send quotes clients accept by OTP, confirm bookings, assign crew and freelancers, upload photos, share a PIN-protected gallery, collect the family's album selection, record client payments and release final photos only after payment. A Today screen shows only the tasks that need action, so nothing slips through.

Is Pikvaa a photo gallery app or a full studio management app?

Pikvaa is both. It covers the full studio workflow, from lead to quote, booking, shoot, upload, gallery, selection, payment, delivery and review. Studios that only need galleries can skip the CRM and use the direct path: create an event, buy a storage pack, share the gallery and deliver, with no quote or booking needed.

Which languages does Pikvaa support?

Pikvaa works in Tamil and English. You choose the language when you sign up, and templates such as the WhatsApp gallery message and landing page are available in both. The app is built first for Tamil Nadu studios, so clients and families can follow the gallery and selection steps in the language they are comfortable with.

Does Pikvaa work on slow internet and low-end phones?

Yes. Working on low-end Android phones and slow networks is one of Pikvaa's core design rules. Uploads resume automatically after a network drop, previews are compressed to web quality of about 0.4 MB, and the Today screen shows a cached view with an 'updated at' time when the connection is weak.

Why use a studio app built just for Indian photographers?

Indian studios usually deliver through Google Drive links, WhatsApp and paper notes, which makes family selection, part-payments and release control messy. Pikvaa is built around Indian realities: rupee pricing, UPI QR payments recorded by the studio, WhatsApp sharing, joint family selection, Tamil and English, phone OTP login and per-event storage packs instead of monthly dollar plans.

Is Pikvaa only for wedding photographers?

No. Weddings are the main use, but Pikvaa works for any event: engagement, pre-wedding, baby and kids, birthdays, fashion and corporate events. Each job is an event with its own storage pack, gallery and PIN. Landing page templates cover wedding, pre-wedding, baby/kids, fashion, events and minimal styles.

Does Pikvaa send WhatsApp messages automatically?

No. Pikvaa never sends automatic WhatsApp messages. After key actions, a 'Share on WhatsApp' button opens your own WhatsApp with an editable Tamil or English template, link and PIN filled in. You decide what goes to the client. SMS through Fast2SMS is used only for OTPs and an optional backup reminder.

Does Pikvaa read my WhatsApp, call logs or contacts?

No. Pikvaa never reads WhatsApp chats, call logs or your phone contacts. On Android you can share a WhatsApp message or call entry into the app to create a lead, and only what you choose to share is taken. Nothing is pulled from your phone in the background.

Getting started

How do I sign up for Pikvaa?

Enter your mobile number, type the 6-digit OTP sent by SMS, then give your studio name and pick Tamil or English. That's it: your studio is created, you become the Owner and a 30-day Pro trial starts with no card needed. You then add and verify a backup email for invoices and account recovery.

Do I need a credit card to start the Pikvaa trial?

No. The 30-day Pro trial needs no card. Sign up with your phone number and OTP and the trial starts at once. One trial is allowed per phone number and per GST number. Storage packs for client events are always paid, even during the trial, and are bought through Razorpay on the web.

What should I set up first after joining Pikvaa?

After signup, a setup checklist suggests your logo, UPI ID, GST number, address, packages and landing page. Nothing is forced. Pikvaa asks for details just in time: your UPI ID before the first client QR, your GST number before the first GST invoice, and a verified email before your first pack payment.

How do I create my first client event?

Create the event with the client's name, shoot date and event price, then pick a storage pack and choose Standard or Protected. Pay once through Razorpay on the web and the exact expiry is locked and shown at purchase. On the Free plan you can do this directly, with no quote or booking needed.

How do I upload photos from memory cards to Pikvaa?

Open the event in the web app on Chrome or Edge, or in the desktop uploader, and pick all files, a subfolder or single files. Pikvaa reserves space in the pack, compresses previews locally and uploads them. Every file is verified, failures retry automatically, and uploads resume after a network drop. Your local files are never deleted.

How do I share the gallery with my client?

Publish the gallery to get one link and one 6-digit Client PIN. Tap 'Share on WhatsApp' and Pikvaa opens your own WhatsApp with a ready Tamil or English message. The link and PIN go as separate messages for safety. You then mark it as shared so it shows on the event timeline.

How long does it take to start using Pikvaa?

Signup is designed to take under a minute: phone number, OTP, studio name and language. You can create an event and buy a pack straight away. Optional details such as logo, UPI ID and GST number can be added later from the setup checklist, only when a step actually needs them.

Can I use Pikvaa without the CRM, quotes or bookings?

Yes. Every studio, including those on the Free plan, can always create an event directly with the client, shoot date and price, record payments against that price and deliver photos. Quotes and bookings are optional CRM tools. You never need a quote to sell a pack or deliver a gallery.

Is there a desktop uploader for large weddings?

Yes. Alongside the web app and the Android and iOS apps, Pikvaa has a small Windows and Mac desktop uploader for moving full memory cards. It uses the same rules as the web uploader: space reserved first, each file verified, automatic retries and resume after network loss, and nothing deleted from your computer.

What is the first step to move my studio onto Pikvaa?

Sign up with your phone number and OTP, add your studio name, then create your next upcoming event directly with the client name, shoot date and price. Buy a pack, upload previews and share the gallery on WhatsApp. You can add past leads and set up quotes, branding and staff later.

Pricing and GST

How much does Pikvaa cost?

Pikvaa has a Free plan, an optional yearly Pro plan and per-event storage packs. Packs start at ₹99 for Lite 3 GB for 6 months, the 40 GB wedding pack is ₹999 for 6 months, and the 120 GB pack is ₹2,999 for 6 months. All prices are before 18% GST, paid once per event.

Are Pikvaa prices inclusive of GST?

No. All Pikvaa prices are listed before 18% GST, and GST is added on the invoice. Checkout shows both the price before GST and the GST-inclusive price, so you see exactly what you pay. For example, the 40 GB wedding pack is ₹999 before GST, about ₹1,179 including GST.

Will I get a GST invoice for Pikvaa payments?

Yes. Every payment you make to Pikvaa is stored with its Razorpay payment ID and an invoice, sent to your verified backup email. Our invoices add 18% GST on top of the listed prices. A verified email is required before your first pack payment, so every invoice reaches you.

How much is Pikvaa Pro?

Pikvaa Pro is priced at ₹4,999 per year plus GST, about ₹417 a month, and the price is still being confirmed with photography studios. Pro adds the full CRM, studio branding, five staff accounts and all landing page templates. Extra staff seats are priced at ₹999 per year each. Storage packs are paid separately.

Is Pikvaa a monthly subscription?

No. There is no monthly fee for storage. You pay once per event for a storage pack sized to that job, for 6 or 12 months. The optional Pro plan, which adds the full CRM, staff and branding, is billed yearly. The Free plan costs nothing and still lets you sell packs and deliver galleries.

What is the cheapest way to deliver one small event on Pikvaa?

The Lite pack is the cheapest option: 3 GB per event for ₹99 for 6 months or ₹149 for 12 months, before GST. It suits small shoots such as birthdays or portraits. Photo count depends on file size, so there is no fixed photo-count promise. You can upgrade later by paying the difference.

Do Razorpay fees get added on top of Pikvaa prices?

No. The Razorpay fee is already included in Pikvaa's pack prices, so you only pay the listed price plus 18% GST. Clients never pay through Razorpay at all; they pay your studio directly, usually by UPI, and you record the payment in the app.

Can Pikvaa prices change?

Pikvaa's launch prices are shaped with five to ten photography studios on real paid events, so they may be adjusted before or after launch. Whatever happens, the exact price and expiry of a pack are locked and shown at purchase, so a pack you have already bought never changes price.

Does Pikvaa offer refunds?

Pro is yearly and part-used years are not refunded if you stop renewing; your account simply moves to Free and nothing is deleted. Packs are paid per event and their exact expiry is shown before you pay. For special cases, platform admins can issue goodwill credit or a pack refund, decided individually.

Why does Pikvaa charge per event instead of unlimited storage?

Unlimited storage is not sustainable, so Pikvaa never offers it. Each pack is priced from real storage costs, and you pay only for the space and months an event needs. A small birthday shoot costs ₹99 while a full wedding with video uses a bigger pack, which keeps prices fair for small studios.

How much is the 50 GB Photos plus Clips pack?

The 50 GB Photos plus Clips plus Final pack costs ₹1,249 for 6 months or ₹2,199 for 12 months before 18% GST, about ₹1,474 including GST for 6 months. Protected versions cost ₹1,718 and ₹3,068. It suits weddings where you deliver short clips along with photos.

What is the extra archive month price?

After expiry, files are archived for 30 days. If more archive time is needed, an extra archive month costs ₹9 for Lite, ₹79 for 40 GB, ₹99 for 50 GB and ₹249 for 120 GB, before GST. Restoring a gallery from the archive costs ₹99 plus an extension.

Packs and storage

What storage packs does Pikvaa offer?

Pikvaa has four packs per event: Lite 3 GB (₹99 for 6 months, ₹149 for 12), Wedding Photos plus Final 40 GB (₹999 / ₹1,799), Photos plus Clips plus Final 50 GB (₹1,249 / ₹2,199) and Full 120 GB (₹2,999 / ₹5,249). Prices are Standard, before 18% GST. Protected versions cost more.

How many photos fit in the 3 GB Lite pack?

It depends on file size, so Pikvaa makes no fixed photo-count promise. The Lite pack gives 3 GB per event. Only what you choose to store counts: web previews of about 0.4 MB each, optional originals and the final package. Storing previews only fits far more photos than storing full originals too.

Which pack should I choose for a wedding?

Most wedding studios start with the 40 GB Wedding pack at ₹999 for 6 months before GST, which covers previews plus the final package. If you also deliver clips, the 50 GB pack fits better, and full event video needs the 120 GB pack. Exact needs depend on your file sizes and what you store.

What is the 120 GB Full pack for?

The 120 GB Full pack is for big events that include full event video along with photos and finals. It costs ₹2,999 for 6 months or ₹5,249 for 12 months before GST, and ₹4,148 / ₹7,348 with protection. Videos up to 30 GB per file can be uploaded within the pack cap.

What counts against my pack's storage?

Only what you choose to store counts: web previews, originals if you store them, clips and video, and the final edited package. Temporary ZIP download files and platform backups never count against your pack. Space is reserved before each upload starts, so parallel uploads can never push you over the cap.

When does a Pikvaa pack start and end?

A pack starts on the shoot date, or the first shoot day for multi-day events, not the purchase date. Its exact expiry is locked and shown when you buy. Each pack includes up to 30 days of storage before the shoot for early uploads, and warnings arrive 30, 7 and 1 days before gallery access ends.

What if the wedding date is rescheduled?

Pikvaa proposes a new expiry based on the new date, but nothing changes until you accept it. A pack can be rescheduled at most twice, to a date up to 6 months out. The pack still starts on the shoot date, and up to 30 days of pre-shoot storage is included.

Can I reuse one pack for several events?

No. Every file belongs to exactly one pack and one event, so one pack cannot be shared across events. If a booking is cancelled, its pack can be reused for another client only after the first client's access is revoked and their photos and records are purged or archived.

What are the file size limits on Pikvaa?

Each photo can be up to 50 MB, each clip up to 500 MB and each full video up to 30 GB. Files over the limit are rejected before upload starts, so you never waste time. All uploads must also fit within your pack's storage cap.

Does Pikvaa store original RAW or JPEG files?

Storing originals is optional. By default Pikvaa stores web-quality previews and the final package. If you choose to store originals, they count against the pack and are kept as one primary copy. Pikvaa does not back up originals, so always keep your own second copy.

Can Free plan studios buy every pack?

Yes. Free studios can buy all storage packs, from Lite 3 GB to Full 120 GB, with no Free-tier pack limits. Packs are always paid on every plan, including Pro and the trial. The Free plan simply limits CRM, staff and branding, not storage or delivery.

Can I store photos for 12 months instead of 6?

Yes. Every pack comes in a 6-month default or a 12-month option. For example, Lite costs ₹149 and the 40 GB Wedding pack ₹1,799 for 12 months, before GST. You can also extend any pack later at ₹5 per GB per month, with a ₹49 minimum.

Can I upload photos before the shoot date?

Yes. Each pack includes up to 30 days of storage before the shoot, useful for pre-wedding shoots or test uploads. Uploading early does not start the pack; it still starts on the shoot date. Storage beyond 30 days before the shoot is charged at the extension rate of ₹5 per GB per month.

Top-ups and upgrades

What happens if my pack runs out of space?

Before an upload starts, Pikvaa shows the exact overflow and offers an upgrade to the next pack or a top-up of extra GB. Nothing is half-uploaded. Lite packs can only upgrade. Top-ups and upgrades keep the same expiry, and the app always shows the final price and reason before you pay.

How much does a storage top-up cost?

A top-up is ₹5 per GB per month left on the pack for Standard packs and ₹6.50 on Protected packs, with a ₹49 minimum, before GST. If the GB's share of the upgrade price is higher, that floor applies instead. The app shows the exact price and why, for example '₹270 (₹5 × 9 GB × 6 months)'.

Can I top up the Lite pack?

No. Top-ups are not available on Lite. Instead, you upgrade Lite to the 40 GB pack by paying the full price difference for the same period, so Lite plus the upgrade always equals buying 40 GB directly: ₹99 plus ₹900 equals ₹999 for 6 months. The expiry date stays the same.

How do pack upgrades work?

To move to the next pack size, you pay the full difference between the two pack prices for the same period, and your expiry stays unchanged. For example, 40 GB to 50 GB costs ₹250 for 6 months before GST. Any unused part of earlier top-ups is credited against the upgrade by actual days remaining.

Is a top-up ever cheaper than upgrading?

No. Pikvaa's pricing makes sure a top-up is never cheaper per GB than upgrading. Top-ups are also capped below the next pack size: 40 GB can add up to 9 GB, 50 GB up to 69 GB and 120 GB up to 30 GB. When upgrading costs the same or less, the app shows 'Upgrade instead' as the main button.

Do I lose money on a top-up if I upgrade later?

No. When you upgrade, the unused part of any top-up is credited against the upgrade price by actual days remaining. Example: a ₹150 top-up bought with 180 days left, upgraded with 90 days left, gives ₹75 credit, so a ₹250 upgrade costs ₹175. Credit is not paid out as cash.

How do I extend a pack for more time?

You can extend any pack before or after expiry at ₹5 per GB of the pack per month before GST, with a ₹49 minimum. An extension moves gallery access, storage expiry and the deletion dates forward together. It does not reopen or move the client's selection deadline; only the studio's reopen can do that.

What if I need more than 150 GB for one event?

The largest pack is 120 GB, and it can be topped up by at most 30 GB. Beyond that, buy a second pack for the event. This keeps prices fair and storage limits clear, since Pikvaa never offers unlimited storage.

Can I restore an expired gallery?

Yes, during the 30-day archive after expiry you can restore an event for ₹99 plus an extension at ₹5 per GB per month. After day 30 files move to a 7-day recovery bin, and on day 37 the live files are permanently deleted. Warnings are sent 30, 7 and 1 days before access ends.

Is a Protected top-up also protected?

Yes. On a Protected pack, top-ups follow the pack's protection and get a verified second copy, priced at ₹6.50 per GB per month with the same upgrade-price floor and ₹49 minimum. On a Standard pack the top-up is not protected, and the screen says so before you pay.

How much does it cost to upgrade from 40 GB to 50 GB?

Upgrading from 40 GB to 50 GB costs the pack price difference for the same period: ₹250 for 6 months or ₹400 for 12 months on Standard, and ₹340 for 6 months on Protected, before GST. Your expiry stays the same, and any unused top-up credit is subtracted.

Can I see the top-up price before I pay?

Yes. Before payment, Pikvaa shows the exact final price and the reason, such as '₹270 (₹5 × 9 GB × 6 months)', '₹125 (upgrade price floor applies)' or '₹49 (minimum charge)'. It also shows the new cap, the unchanged expiry and whether the top-up is protected.

Protection and backups

What is the difference between Standard and Protected storage?

Standard keeps one primary copy of your final photos plus a 7-day recovery bin. Protected adds a verified second copy of final photos and clips on a separate provider for the paid period and archive month, with restore within 24 hours. Both prices are shown side by side at checkout and nothing is pre-selected.

How much does Protected storage cost?

Protected packs cost: Lite ₹129 for 6 months and ₹209 for 12; 40 GB ₹1,378 / ₹2,498; 50 GB ₹1,718 / ₹3,068; 120 GB ₹4,148 / ₹7,348. All prices are before 18% GST. Protection is optional and never pre-selected; you choose Standard or Protected yourself at checkout.

Does Pikvaa back up my photos?

It depends on the file type. Final photos and clips get a verified second copy only on Protected packs. Previews have a 7-day recovery bin, with no second copy. Originals are kept as one primary copy and are not backed up, so keep your own copy. Pikvaa states this plainly instead of promising everything.

Should I keep my memory cards after uploading to Pikvaa?

Yes. Keep your cards until you have your own independent second copy, such as on your own drive. Pikvaa shows a keep-your-cards warning until you confirm that copy, because originals are stored as one copy and not backed up. Studios storing previews only see 'Keep your cards. Originals are not stored here.'

What does 'Protected' status mean on an event?

'Protected' appears only after the second copy of your final photos and clips is verified by checksum. Until then the event shows 'Protection in progress'. If a copy fails, Pikvaa retries automatically and alerts you if it keeps failing. Protection follows pack extensions, and restores happen within 24 hours.

What happens if I delete photos by mistake?

Deleted files go to a 7-day recovery bin first, so you can restore them within 7 days. After that they are purged. On Protected packs, final photos and clips also have a verified second copy that can be restored within 24 hours. Previews lost after 7 days can be re-uploaded from your cards.

How does Pikvaa protect my studio's data like bookings and payments?

Your studio data sits in a managed database with 7-day point-in-time restore, daily backups kept for 30 days and an encrypted daily off-site copy at a different provider. A monthly automated test restores a full event, including photos, selections and permissions, to prove backups actually work.

What does Protected storage not cover?

Protection covers final photos and clips, plus their top-ups. It does not cover previews or originals, which have only the 7-day recovery bin. This is shown on the Protected card at checkout, so you know exactly what you are paying for before choosing.

Where are photos stored on Pikvaa?

Photos are stored on Cloudflare R2 object storage, with archive months on R2 Infrequent Access. On Protected packs, a second copy of final photos and clips is kept on Backblaze B2, a separate provider. Pikvaa plans to move primary storage to Backblaze B2 as volume grows.

What happens to my galleries if Pikvaa has an outage?

Pikvaa monitoring alerts the team within 5 minutes, a public status page and in-app banner keep you informed, and client galleries are kept working where possible. Client time periods such as selection deadlines and pack warnings are extended by the length of the outage, and affected studios get an incident note.

Client selection and gallery

How does client photo selection work in Pikvaa?

The client opens the gallery link, enters the Client PIN and tags photos as Love, Favourite or Add to album. 'Add to album' is the selection and counts toward the album limit you set. Album photos can get priority 1, 2 or 3. The client submits with an OTP, and the selection locks for editing.

Can the whole family select wedding photos together?

Yes. Everyone with the Client PIN works on one shared live selection that syncs instantly. If someone removes a photo, others see 'removed by' with their name and an Undo button, so nothing disappears silently. Only the main client can submit the final selection, confirmed by OTP on their phone.

How long does the client have to select photos?

The selection deadline is 60 days from when the gallery goes live, with reminders at 30, 7 and 1 days. Each studio reopen adds at most 14 days, with a hard cap of 120 days. After the deadline, selection closes, but the gallery stays viewable until the pack ends.

Can the client change the selection after submitting?

Not directly. After submit, the selection is locked. To change it, the client asks the studio, and only the studio can reopen it. Studios set how many free reopens they give and may charge for corrections after that. Every resubmission becomes a new version, with older versions and a diff kept.

How do I get clients to select album photos faster?

Pikvaa sets a clear 60-day selection deadline with automatic reminders at 30, 7 and 1 days, lets the whole family select together on one live list, and uses simple tags plus album priority 1, 2 and 3. An album limit counter shows how many photos remain, which helps families decide sooner.

How do wedding photographers send photos to clients for selection?

Many Indian studios share a Google Drive folder and collect choices over WhatsApp, which gets messy. With Pikvaa, the studio publishes a PIN-protected gallery of watermarked web previews, shares the link and PIN on WhatsApp, and the family tags and submits their album selection in one place, confirmed by OTP.

Can the bride's and groom's families select photos separately?

In Pikvaa, everyone with the Client PIN works on one shared live selection, and each removal shows who made it with Undo, so both families see one list. For separate viewing, the client can create up to 10 share galleries with view-only PINs, where relatives give hearts that never change the selection.

What are hearts in a Pikvaa share gallery?

Hearts let relatives and friends show which photos they love without changing the album. Share-gallery viewers enter their name once and can give one heart per photo. The studio and client can sort by most loved; viewers cannot see counts. Hearts are a guide only and never change the selection.

What is a share gallery?

A share gallery is a curated, view-only gallery the client creates from their own gallery to show chosen photos to relatives and friends. Pikvaa generates a separate view-only PIN for it. The client can create up to 10 per event and rename, edit, disable or delete them at any time.

Can the studio set an album photo limit?

Yes. The studio sets an album limit, and every photo tagged 'Add to album' counts toward it. When the limit is reached, Pikvaa blocks further additions and shows the count, so families know exactly how many photos they can still pick. The editor then sees the selection sorted by album priority.

How does the editor see the client's selection?

When the client submits, the selection appears in the studio's 'Ready for you' card. The editor sees the submitted photos sorted by album priority 1, 2 and 3, along with Love and Favourite tags. If the selection is reopened and resubmitted, each version is kept with a diff showing what changed.

Does the client need to install an app to view photos?

No. Clients use a web link only, with no app install. They open the link the studio sends on WhatsApp, enter the 6-digit Client PIN, and can view, tag, select and submit photos from any phone browser. Studio owners and staff use the web, Android or iOS apps.

What happens when the gallery is published late?

If fewer than 60 days remain on the pack when the gallery goes live, the studio must either extend the pack or accept a shorter selection deadline on screen before publishing. The client always sees the real deadline, so nobody is surprised by a gallery closing early.

Does closing the selection close the gallery?

No. Closing the selection never shortens paid gallery access. After the selection deadline, the client can still view the gallery until the pack ends. Pikvaa tracks four separate dates per event: selection deadline, gallery access end, storage expiry and permanent deletion, all shown on the event page.

Can I turn the watermark off for a client?

Yes. Watermarks are on by default, and the studio can turn them off per event. The watermark is added on Pikvaa's server, and changing it reprocesses the previews. When the event watermark is on, previews in share galleries are always watermarked too.

Can clients download photos before paying?

Before payment, Client PIN holders can download a limited number of social-size copies, around 2048 px with a small studio logo, called the share quota. The default is 10 per event, and the studio can add more or sell extras. Full-quality files are released only after payment and studio approval.

Can I show my UPI QR in the client gallery?

Yes. The gallery can show your studio's own UPI QR with the balance prefilled. The client pays you directly, then you check your bank and mark the payment received in Pikvaa. Pikvaa never handles client money and never claims money arrived; only the studio confirms it.

Does Pikvaa send a reminder if the client doesn't open the gallery?

Yes, optionally. If the gallery is still unopened after 3 days, Pikvaa can send a backup SMS. Otherwise, all client messages go through your own WhatsApp using the 'Share on WhatsApp' button, so the studio stays in control of every message sent.

PIN and security

What is a Client PIN?

A Client PIN is the 6-digit PIN the studio shares with the main client. It gives full rights: view, select, submit, download the share quota, create share galleries and request deletion. The studio shares the gallery link and PIN as separate WhatsApp messages, and can change the PIN at any time.

What happens if someone guesses the gallery PIN wrong?

After 5 wrong PIN attempts, the gallery locks for 15 minutes. This applies to the Client PIN and to share-gallery PINs, which makes guessing impractical. If a PIN leaks, the studio can change the Client PIN, and the old one stops working at once.

What if my gallery PIN is leaked?

The studio can change the Client PIN, and every session using the old PIN ends immediately. If a share-gallery PIN leaks, the client can disable that share gallery, or the studio can disable all share galleries for the event in one tap. Active downloads on revoked access are also cut.

Can people take full-quality photos from the gallery without paying?

No. Galleries show only web-quality watermarked previews, and full-quality files are never reachable through any gallery or share link. Full quality is released only after the studio confirms payment and approves the exact final files. Right-click and long-press are blocked, though no app can honestly promise screenshots are blocked.

Can Pikvaa stop clients taking screenshots?

No, and Pikvaa never promises that. Pikvaa blocks right-click and long-press, shows only watermarked web-quality previews, and never exposes full-quality files before release. A screenshot captures only a low-resolution watermarked image, so your finished work stays protected until payment. Clean files arrive only after release.

Why does Pikvaa send an OTP when the client submits?

The OTP goes to the main client's phone so that only they can lock the final selection. A relative cannot submit without the client knowing. The OTP record, with time and version, also gives the studio proof of what was chosen if there is ever a dispute.

How does Pikvaa keep my studio account secure?

Login uses phone OTP. Each person can have at most 3 active devices, and the Owner is alerted on any new device login. Sensitive actions such as payments, release and deletion ask for a fresh OTP if the last check was over 12 hours ago. Idle sessions log out after 60 days.

What if my phone is lost or stolen?

Remove the device from Settings, Devices and its session ends at once. A stolen phone used offline is rejected the next time it reaches the server. If you lose your number, a recovery request checks ID proof, waits 72 hours with notices to the old number and email, and is reviewed by the platform.

Can my staff see client phone numbers and payments?

Only if their role allows it. Photographers see their assigned events and schedule but no amounts or client phone numbers. Editors upload finals and see selections but cannot publish or release. Freelancers never see payments, the CRM or client phone numbers. Billing and deletion are Owner-only.

Can other studios see my clients or photos?

No. Each studio's data is isolated, and cross-studio isolation is tested for every gallery, download, API and background job.
