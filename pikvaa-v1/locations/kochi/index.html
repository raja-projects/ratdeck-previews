What is a good photo selection and delivery app for photographers in Kochi?

Pikvaa is a studio app for photographers in Kochi and across South India: families select album photos together in a private PIN gallery, you record part-payments, and full-quality photos are released once the job is paid. Packs start at ₹99 + GST per event.

Peak-season crews

Invite freelancers to individual events. They see only their assignment, never your amounts or client numbers, and don't count as paid seats.

Quotes that stick

Send quotes as a PDF and link; the client accepts by OTP. Tentative bookings expire after hold days if no advance arrives, freeing the date.

Destination and multi-day work

For multi-day events, the pack starts on the first shoot day. Use 120 GB for large multi-day weddings with clips.

Yes. If a tentative booking's hold days pass without an advance, the date is freed.
