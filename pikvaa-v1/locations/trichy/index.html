What is a good photo selection and delivery app for photographers in Tiruchirappalli?

Pikvaa is a studio app for photographers in Tiruchirappalli and across Tamil Nadu: families select album photos together in a private PIN gallery, you record part-payments, and full-quality photos are released once the job is paid. Packs start at ₹99 + GST per event.

Built for patchy networks

Galleries show web-quality previews of around 0.4 MB, so families can browse and select on slower connections. Your uploads from the desktop upload app resume after a network drop or a laptop going to sleep.

Collecting the balance

Payments in many studios arrive in parts - advance, on the day, and after the album. Record each part, and final downloads unlock only when payments reach the approved price.

Small studios, small jobs

Birthday and baby shoots can use the 3 GB Lite pack at ₹99 + GST for 6 months, keeping delivery costs in proportion to the job.
