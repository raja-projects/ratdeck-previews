What is a good photo selection and delivery app for photographers in Tirunelveli?

Pikvaa is a studio app for photographers in Tirunelveli and across Tamil Nadu: families select album photos together in a private PIN gallery, you record part-payments, and full-quality photos are released once the job is paid. Packs start at ₹99 + GST per event.

Relatives far from home

The main client can create up to 10 share galleries, each with a view-only PIN, for relatives in other cities or abroad. They can view and heart photos; only Client-PIN holders select and submit.

Dates people understand

Selection deadlines and gallery end dates appear in IST and in the viewer's local time.

Keeping control

If a PIN is forwarded too widely, change it - old sessions end immediately. You can disable all share galleries for an event in one tap.
