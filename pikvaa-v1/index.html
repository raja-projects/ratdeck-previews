What is Pikvaa?

Pikvaa is a studio app for Indian photographers that runs the whole job in one place: leads, quotes, bookings, crew, upload, client gallery and photo selection, payment records and final delivery. It is designed for solo photographers and small studios of two to five people, works in Tamil and English, and is built for everyday Android phones and slow networks.

Is Pikvaa free to use?

Pikvaa has a Free plan for running events and delivering photos, an optional yearly Pro plan for the full CRM, staff and branding, and paid per-event storage packs starting at ₹99 before GST.

Do my clients need to install an app?

No. Clients open the gallery from a link in any browser and enter the 6-digit Client PIN you share with them.

Are Pikvaa prices inclusive of GST?

No. Prices are listed before 18% GST. Checkout shows both the price before GST and the GST-inclusive price, for example ₹999 for the 40 GB wedding pack, about ₹1,179 including GST.

Can I use Pikvaa in Tamil?

Yes. Pikvaa works in Tamil and English and is being launched first with studios in Tamil Nadu.
