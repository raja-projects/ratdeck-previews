What software does a small photography studio need?

A small studio needs one place for enquiries and quotes, a booking calendar that reserves crew, payment records per event, and a client gallery for selection and delivery. Pikvaa combines these with role-based access, so the owner sees everything and photographers see only their own schedule.

Team

Roles that match a real studio.

Owner, Manager, Photographer and Editor roles come built in, or create custom roles. Billing, deletion and ownership transfer always stay with the Owner.
