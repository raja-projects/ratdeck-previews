What is the best app for wedding photographers in India?

Choose an app that lets the whole family select album photos together, keeps previews watermarked, records part-payments by UPI and cash, and releases full-quality photos only when paid. A typical wedding on Pikvaa

  1. Enquiry arrives on WhatsApp — add it as a lead and send a quote.
  2. The couple accepts by OTP; you mark the advance and the date is confirmed.
  3. Assign your team and a freelancer for the wedding day.
  4. Upload previews with the desktop upload app; publish and share the link and Client PIN.
  5. The family selects and the main client submits by OTP.
  6. Record the balance; release finals for download.
