A Kwikpic alternative for album selection and studio work

Kwikpic and Pikvaa solve different problems. Kwikpic is built around AI face search, so guests find their own photos. Pikvaa is built around the studio job: album selection by the family, watermarked previews, payment records and final delivery.

Is Pikvaa a good Kwikpic alternative in India?

It depends on your need. If you want guests at an event to find their own photos by face, Kwikpic's AI face search is designed for that and Pikvaa does not offer it. If you need the family to select album photos, track part-payments and release finals after payment - plus bookings and crew - Pikvaa is the closer fit.

Choose by the job

Large events with many guests who each want their own photos - corporate events, college functions, big receptions - are where face search shines. That is Kwikpic's focus.

Wedding and family jobs where the deliverable is an album and the family must agree on a list, and where the studio needs to be paid before releasing finals, are where Pikvaa focuses.

Some studios may use both: a face-search app for guest sharing at a reception, and Pikvaa for the couple's album selection and final delivery.

Pricing model

Kwikpic's public pages show a free tier of 1,000 photos and yearly plans. Pikvaa charges per event, so cost scales with how many jobs you shoot. Compare against your own yearly volume using our pricing page.

FAQs

Is Pikvaa a good Kwikpic alternative in India?

It depends on your need. Kwikpic focuses on AI face-search event photo sharing, where guests find their own photos. Pikvaa focuses on the studio workflow: album selection by the family, watermarked previews, payment records and release only after payment, plus CRM, crew and bookings. Pikvaa does not offer face search.

Does Pikvaa have AI face search like some photo sharing apps?

No. AI face search is not part of Pikvaa's launch features. Pikvaa focuses on album selection by the client's family, watermarked previews, payment-linked release, bookings and crew. If guests finding their own photos by selfie is your main need, a face-search app may suit that job better.

Does Pikvaa offer AI photo culling?

No, AI culling is not part of Pikvaa. You choose which photos to upload from your cards, and the client's family selects album photos with tags and priorities. Pikvaa focuses on selection, payment and delivery rather than editing or culling.
