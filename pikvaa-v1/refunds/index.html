Billing

Refund Policy

We review billing problems using the purchase, provisioning and service records linked to your Pikvaa account.

Effective date: 2 November 2026.

Eligible reviews

Contact Pikvaa if you were charged more than once for the same purchase, payment succeeded but the plan or event pack was not provisioned, or a verified Pikvaa service problem prevented meaningful use and support could not restore it.

Situations that do not normally qualify

A change of mind after use, unused capacity, missed client deadlines, expired storage, files deleted after the displayed recovery period, failure to keep originals, or problems outside Pikvaa's control do not normally qualify. Rights that apply under law remain unaffected.

Request a review

Open Help in your Pikvaa account, choose Contact support, then Billing. Include the studio name, transaction reference, purchase date and a concise description. Never send payment passwords, one-time passwords or full card details.

Outcome

We may verify account ownership, payment status, provisioning and usage. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where possible. Payment-provider processing times may apply. Final request and processing timelines must be confirmed before publication.

Cancellations

Cancelling renewal stops the next renewal charge. It does not automatically refund the current subscription or an event pack already provisioned.

Contact

Use the billing channel on the Contact page. The official billing address and business operator details must be inserted before publication.
