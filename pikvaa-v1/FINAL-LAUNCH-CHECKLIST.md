# Pikvaa launch checklist

This working branch contains launch-ready marketing copy and UI. It is not deployed.

The following business and legal facts must be confirmed before publishing:

- Legal operator name and registered or principal business address
- Official support, privacy, grievance, and legal contact email addresses
- Grievance officer name and escalation response times
- Final payment processor, hosting, storage, analytics, messaging, and support vendors
- Exact production data locations, backup practices, and retention schedules
- Final cancellation and refund response timelines
- Legal review for the Terms, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy
- Production validation for uploads, client sharing, quote acceptance, payments, and downloads
- Replacement of GitHub Pages preview paths with the production domain paths

Do not publish the branch until every item above is verified.
