Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

This is a draft placeholder summarising intended practices. It is not a final legal document and is not yet in force.

DRAFT — placeholder only. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Do not rely on this page.

  • What we collect: studio account details (name, phone, email, GST number), event and client records you enter, photos you upload, payment records, device and session data.
  • How photos are handled: previews are watermarked by default; full-quality files are reachable only after release, through a controlled download gateway.
  • Retention: after pack expiry, files are archived for 30 days, then held in a 7-day recovery bin; live files are deleted by day 37 and from backups by day 67. Audit logs are kept 1 year; payment and invoice logs 8 years, with no photos.
  • Client deletion requests: clients can request deletion; verified requests follow a reviewed process.
  • Support access: only when the studio grants it, for 1 hour, 24 hours or 3 days.
  • Data location: storage providers may not keep all data in India; this is being reviewed.
  • Breaches: we follow a written response plan and notify affected users and authorities as required by law.
  • [To be completed and reviewed by an Indian privacy lawyer before launch.]
