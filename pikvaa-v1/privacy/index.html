Privacy Policy

What information Pikvaa handles, why it is used and the choices available to users.

Effective date: 2 November 2026.

Information we handle

Pikvaa may handle studio account details, staff and freelancer details, event and client records, photographs and gallery activity, quotes, bookings, payment records entered by the studio, support messages, device information, sessions and security logs.

How we use it

We use information to provide accounts and role-based access, operate event workflows, process and deliver photos, record selections, support bookings and payment records, provide support, secure the service and meet legal obligations.

Studio responsibilities

The studio decides which client and event information it enters and who receives gallery access. Studios are responsible for providing notices and choices required for their photography services.

Sharing

Information may be processed by providers supporting hosting, storage, authentication, payments, messaging, monitoring and support. We may disclose information when required by law or needed to protect users and the service. The final provider list and processing locations must be verified before publication.

Retention

Event screens show selection deadlines, gallery end dates, storage expiry and deletion dates. Account, invoice, security and support records may be retained longer for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes. Final production periods must be confirmed before publication.

Security

We use access controls and operational safeguards designed for the information handled. No system is completely secure. Protect account credentials and gallery PINs, and keep original photographs separately.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or another available privacy right. We may verify identity and may direct a gallery visitor to the studio controlling the event record.

Children and changes

Studios must obtain required permissions before uploading or sharing photographs of children. Material policy changes will be posted with an updated effective date.

Contact

Use the instructions on the Contact page. The legal operator, privacy contact, grievance officer, provider list and final retention schedule must be inserted and reviewed before publication.
