Wedding photography studio management software: a practical guide

Pikvaa team · Published 1 November 2026 · 3 min read

How Indian wedding studios can manage leads, quotes, bookings, crew, payments and delivery in one place, and what to look for in studio management software.

The short answer: wedding studio management software should follow the job from enquiry to album: capture leads, send quotes clients accept, confirm bookings with an advance, assign crew, record part-payments and deliver. If it doesn't connect those steps, you'll still be copying between notebooks, spreadsheets and WhatsApp.

Many Indian studios run on a mix of a paper diary, a WhatsApp group for the crew, a spreadsheet of payments and Google Drive for delivery. It works until the busy season, when a double-booking or a forgotten balance costs real money. Here is how to think about studio management software.

The stages of a wedding job

Enquiry. A couple messages on Instagram or WhatsApp asking for packages. You need to record the lead with dates and requirements, and follow up.

Quote. You send a package with price and coverage. Without a record, disputes about “what was included” are common months later.

Booking. The couple pays an advance and the date is confirmed. Until then the date is only tentatively held.

Crew. You assign staff and, in peak season, freelancers. Each person needs to know where and when — but not necessarily the price or the client's phone number.

Shoot and upload. Cards are handed over, previews uploaded, originals copied safely.

Selection. The family chooses album photos.

Payments. Balances arrive in parts by UPI, cash or bank transfer.

Delivery. Finals are released, ideally after the balance is cleared.

What to look for

Leads that don't get lost

Quick capture from your phone matters more than complex pipelines. Pikvaa supports quick add, sharing a contact into the app and enquiries from your studio landing page, with simple stages like New and Talking. Duplicate leads are flagged rather than merged automatically, so you don't lose context.

Quotes with a clear acceptance

A quote the client accepts formally protects both sides. In Pikvaa you send a quote as a PDF and link, and the client accepts by OTP. Quotes expire after their validity period.

Bookings tied to advances

An accepted quote becomes a tentative booking with hold days. Marking the advance received confirms it and reserves crew and shoot hours. If the hold passes without an advance, the date is freed — no more dates blocked by couples who went elsewhere.

Roles and privacy for your team

Not everyone needs to see everything. Pikvaa's roles — Owner, Manager, Photographer, Editor and custom roles — mean photographers see their assignments but not amounts or client phone numbers. Billing, deletion and ownership transfer stay with the Owner. Removing someone ends their access on every device.

Freelancers without extra cost

Freelancers are a normal part of Indian wedding coverage. Pikvaa lets you invite them per event at no seat cost, with access limited to their assignment.

Payments recorded against the job

Record every part-payment against the event and see what's due. Payment and invoice logs should be kept for years for accounting; Pikvaa keeps them for eight.

Delivery in the same system

When delivery lives in the same tool, release can follow payments automatically: finals unlock when confirmed payments reach the approved price.

Free versus paid

Solo photographers may not need a full CRM. Pikvaa's Free plan lets an owner create events, sell storage packs, record payments and deliver. Pro, planned at ₹4,999 a year plus GST and still being confirmed with pilot studios, adds the full CRM, studio branding and five staff accounts.

Getting started without disruption

  1. Start with new enquiries only; don't migrate old jobs.
  2. Add your team with the right roles.
  3. Run one upcoming wedding end to end.
  4. Review what worked before the busy season.
