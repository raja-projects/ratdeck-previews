About StorySpring

Mission-driven organizations are changing lives. Our team of filmmakers, designers, writers and strategists helps you tell the world who you are, why you matter, and what you need to thrive.

StorySpring believes that stories inspire change. We help your organization articulate and tell its story so your audiences know who you are, why you matter, and what your organization needs to thrive.

StorySpring is based in Denver, Colorado, with team members contributing from across the country to help us do our best and brightest work. Working with StorySpring means your team just got bigger.

How we tell a story

Eight ways in.

Everything under one (virtual) roof, so the website, the film, the magazine and the launch event all sound like you.

01

Web Design + Development

A website is more than a digital presence, it's a storytelling tool. We design and build websites that intuitively and artfully communicate your organization's unique offerings and accomplishments.

02

Video Production

Your work, in action. From concept to final cut, we combine moving image, documentary storytelling, testimonials, and music to make an evocative, versatile piece.

03

Writing + Messaging Strategy

Ever notice how one word has the power to either rub or resonate? We craft succinct messaging that doesn't just stick but sings.

04

Print + Publication Design

Give it away! Publications that are as informative and entertaining as they are beautiful, keeping your message front-and-center long after the meeting.

05

Brand Development

Your brand is the doorway into your work. Let's make it one everyone wants to walk through: name, logo, typography, visuals and voice, working together.

06

Executive Public Image

Leaders are the face of an organization, and therefore its primary storyteller. We shape presence and messaging to strengthen influence and deepen engagement.

07

Group Facilitation + Community Building

Facilitation that fosters connection and clarity, so your team, stakeholders, and community members move forward with alignment and purpose.

08

Events

An event is a story unfolding in real time. Want to shake up what a nonprofit fundraiser can look and feel like? Us too. From concept and catering to keynote and clean-up.
