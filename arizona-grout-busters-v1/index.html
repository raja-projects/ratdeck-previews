What we do

Every kind of tile, made new again.

We specialize in the repair and maintenance of tile and grout. Floors, showers, countertops and patios, for homes across the Tucson area.

Our specialty

Saltillo strip & reseal

Old sealer comes off with a solvent stripper, by hand, so the new sealer bonds properly. Then multiple coats go on for a consistent shine.

  • High shine or matte
  • Clean & seal without stripping
  • Pop/lime repair
  • Tile replacement

Grout cleaning & sealing

Hand cleaned to pull dirt out of the joints, then a clear penetrating sealer to keep it out.

Color change grout

Bring grout back to its original color, or change it entirely, without removing it. Sealed at the same time.

Regrout showers & counters

Old grout and caulk out, new in. Done in one day, and the shower is yours again within 24 hours.

Natural stone

Travertine, flagstone patios, steps and entries, cleaned, color enhanced and sealed.

Cracked tile replaced

We cut and match replacement tile and grout color so the repair disappears.

Caulking

Moldy caulk removed and replaced around tubs, showers, sinks and counters, with care instructions after.

✕We do not use re-glazing or “quick fix” methods. Every job is professionally hand cleaned and sealed.
How Saltillo is done right

Yes, it takes longer. It's worth it.

Machines can't get old sealer out of grout lines and corners, and Saltillo clay is too soft for them. So we don't use them on it.
