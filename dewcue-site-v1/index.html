Questions

Short answers. The privacy policy has the full account of what is stored.

Does Dewcue read my email?

No. There is no email sign-in and no mailbox access. A follow-up uses the subject, sender name, and link already on screen, and only when you ask. That stays on your computer.

Where do my reminders live?

On your computer. Upcoming and overdue reminders can also sync through Chrome to your other computers. Buckets, the timer, nudges, and history stay on this computer. Dewcue does not upload your pages.

Does the card read the page I am on?

No. On-page cards are drawn on top of the page. Dewcue does not read or store that page. A title, address, or text you highlight is saved only when you use the toolbar or the right-click menu and ask it to.

How does the trial work?

The first time you pass a free limit, a 14-day Premium trial starts on that computer. No card is required. After the trial, the free limits return unless you upgrade.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for. The refund page explains the 7-day window and how money goes back through Razorpay.

Which price will I see?

Dewcue shows Indian prices, plus GST, when your locale is en-IN or your timezone is Asia/Kolkata. Otherwise it shows US dollars. You can switch on the upgrade screen.
