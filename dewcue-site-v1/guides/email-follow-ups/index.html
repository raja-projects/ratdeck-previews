How to follow up on an email without keeping it in your head

Save the conversation you want to revisit, choose a useful time, and check the reply yourself.

DewCue guides·3 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

In this guide

The useful answer

Choose an email, start its capture in DewCue, and schedule a time to return. When the reminder arrives, open the conversation and check whether you need to reply, follow up, or mark the task done. DewCue does not monitor replies or send messages for you.

Save the conversation you actually need

Use email follow-ups for a proposal awaiting feedback, a document you requested, or a question that needs an answer. Choose a specific next step so the reminder remains useful when it appears.

Capture is manual and user initiated. DewCue can save the email subject, sender name, and visible link. It does not ask for your email sign-in or access to your mailbox.

  1. Open the relevant conversation in your webmail.
  2. Start the email capture in DewCue and check the saved context.
  3. Choose when you want to return to the conversation.

Check the conversation before you act

When the reminder arrives, read the current conversation. A reply may already be waiting. If the next step is complete, mark the reminder done. If you need more time, snooze it or ask to be reminded again.

The Premium no-reply follow-up workflow still depends on your review. It does not automatically detect replies, inspect unread mail, or send a follow-up email.

Keep enough context for the next check

For client work, keep a short update in a bucket: what you requested, what you are waiting for, and the next action. Bucket notes stay local.

The privacy policy documents this capture workflow on Gmail, Outlook on the web, and Yahoo Mail. Free includes ten captured emails per month; Premium removes that Free plan limit.

A few related questions

How do email follow-ups work in DewCue?
You choose an email to capture and set a time to return to it. DewCue can save its subject, sender name, and visible link after you start the capture. At the scheduled time, the reminder prompts you to open the conversation and check what needs to happen next.
Create a useful email follow-up
Does DewCue automatically detect whether someone has replied?
No. A scheduled follow-up asks you to check the conversation yourself. DewCue does not monitor your mailbox or automatically determine reply status. The Premium no-reply follow-up workflow helps you return to the email and decide whether another follow-up is needed.
Follow up without mailbox monitoring
What email information does DewCue save?
On supported webmail pages, DewCue can use the visible subject, sender name, and message link after you allow it on that site. It does not read the message body or inbox list, ask for email sign-in, or send email information to a DewCue server.
Review email capture details
Will DewCue send emails for me?
No. DewCue reminds you to revisit a conversation. You read the email, decide whether a reply is needed, and send any message yourself through your email service.
