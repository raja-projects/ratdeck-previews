How to check DewCue reminders and browser notifications

Understand the two alert styles and run a small test before relying on a reminder.

DewCue guides·3 min read·Documentation reviewed 18 October 2026

In this guide

The useful answer

Choose on-page cards or system notifications, check the permission needed for that option, and create a short test with Chrome open and your computer awake. DewCue does not guarantee alerts when Chrome, the computer, or the network is off. Keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks.

Check which alert you selected

On-page cards appear in your browsing space. System notifications use the computer's notification display. Start by checking the option you selected in DewCue so you know where to look.

On-page cards request permission when you enable them. Displaying the card does not read the page. If you choose system notifications, check whether your operating system allows Chrome to show alerts.

Run one small, observable test

Use a low-stakes test instead of an important deadline. Keep the browser open, leave the computer awake, and note whether the alert appears where expected.

  1. Create a reminder a few minutes ahead and check its saved date and time.
  2. Confirm the selected alert style and any requested permission.
  3. Check your operating system's notification and do-not-disturb settings.
  4. Observe the test, then mark it done in your reminder list.

Know what a successful test tells you

A successful test confirms the conditions you just checked. It does not establish delivery with Chrome closed, the computer asleep, or different operating-system settings. DewCue does not promise every alarm will fire when Chrome, the computer, or the network is off.

Use a separate backup alarm for time-critical commitments. When you return to the computer, review Today and Overdue for tasks that still need attention. If optional wellbeing nudges are quiet, also check their quiet hours and mute setting.

A few related questions

Where will my reminders appear?
You can choose on-page reminder cards or system notifications. On-page cards need permission when you enable that option. For system notifications, also check whether your operating system allows Chrome to display alerts.
Choose and check your alerts
Will reminders arrive when Chrome is closed or my computer is asleep?
Do not rely on an alert in those conditions. DewCue does not guarantee that every alarm will fire when Chrome, the computer, or the network is off. Test your actual setup, keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks, and review Today and Overdue when you return.
Test your notification setup
Can I quiet the wellbeing nudges during meetings or after work?
Yes. Wellbeing nudges have quiet hours and a mute option so you can reduce interruptions during meetings or after work. Quiet-hour settings can sync through Chrome; the wellbeing nudge settings themselves stay on the computer you use.
Choose when nudges are useful
How are my reminders organized?
DewCue separates reminders into Today, Upcoming, and Overdue. Today helps you see what needs attention now, Upcoming shows what is ahead, and Overdue helps you revisit unfinished reminders. The side panel shows the same reminder list.
