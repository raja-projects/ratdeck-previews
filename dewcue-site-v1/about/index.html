About DewCue

The ideaClear boundariesThe business

A small place for the next step.

A reply to check. A client detail to keep. A little water between two busy hours. DewCue brings those moments into your browser with natural-language reminders, email follow-up prompts, project buckets and timers.

Today, Upcoming and Overdue help give reminders a place. On-page cards or system notifications bring them back when it is time. The side panel gives the same list a little more room.

Useful help, with clear boundaries.

You do not need a DewCue account for reminders. Email follow-ups do not require mailbox sign-in or send messages for you. Reminders and selected preferences can sync through Chrome; buckets, notes, timers and history stay on your computer.

Premium licence records are separate. The licence service stores the email you enter, licence status and an installation ID. The privacy policy explains what is stored and where.

Who’s behind DewCue?

DewCue is offered by Tanishkha, a sole proprietorship operated by Anisha Rajamurugan in Kanniyakumari, Tamil Nadu, India. The full published business details are available in the Terms of Service.
