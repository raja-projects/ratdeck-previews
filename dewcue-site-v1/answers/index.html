Getting started

What is DewCue?
DewCue is a Chrome extension for reminders, email follow-ups, timers, and optional wellbeing nudges. It brings your Today, Upcoming, and Overdue lists into your browser, with a side panel for the same reminders while you work.
Set your first reminder
Do I need an account to create reminders?
No. You can create reminders without creating a DewCue account. Premium activation is separate: it uses the email address you enter, your license status, and an installation ID for the computer you activate.
Read the getting-started guide
Which browser does DewCue support?
DewCue is designed as a Chrome extension. Support for other browsers, a mobile app, and a standalone desktop app has not been confirmed. Check extension availability before planning your setup.
Check the starting steps
Can I type a reminder in everyday English?
Yes. DewCue accepts natural-language reminders such as “call mom tomorrow at 6” and “rent on the 5th.” Use a clear task, date, and time, then check the saved schedule so it matches what you meant.
Write a clear reminder

Browsing & capture

Can I save a web page to revisit with a reminder?
Yes. When you choose the DewCue toolbar or right-click capture action, DewCue can save the page title, web address, and any selected text. Give the reminder a clear action and check its saved date and time. The captured page copy stays on the computer where you saved it.
Save a page with useful context
Can I turn highlighted text into a reminder?
Yes. Select the text you want to keep and use DewCue’s toolbar or right-click capture action. DewCue saves the selected text with the page title and address because you requested the capture. Check that the saved text contains enough context to understand the task later.
Use selected text in a reminder
Does capturing a page save a complete offline copy of the website?
The documented capture saves a page title, web address, and selected text. It should not be treated as a full-page archive. Keep enough context in the reminder to recognize the task, and check the original page when you return to it.
Understand what a page capture keeps
Will captured page text appear on my other computer?
The saved copy of a page title, address, and selected text stays on the computer where you captured it. Reminder syncing is a separate part of DewCue. Do not assume that a reminder appearing on another computer means the locally captured page information has transferred too.
Check what moves between computers
Can I see my reminders beside the page I am working on?
Yes. DewCue’s Chrome side panel puts the same reminder list beside your current page with more room to view it. You do not need to create a separate set of reminders for the panel. Review Today, Upcoming, and Overdue to choose your next action.
Use the side panel while browsing

Reminders

Where will my reminders appear?
You can choose on-page reminder cards or system notifications. On-page cards need permission when you enable that option. For system notifications, also check whether your operating system allows Chrome to display alerts.
Choose and check your alerts
What can I do when a reminder appears?
Mark it done when the task is finished, snooze it when you need more time, or use remind again when it still needs your attention. Choose the action that reflects the task's actual status to keep your list useful.
Build a simple reminder routine
Can I create repeating reminders?
Yes. Repeating reminders are part of DewCue. The Free plan includes five repeat reminders, and Premium removes that Free plan limit. Check the saved repeat schedule before relying on it for a recurring task.
Compare plan limits
How are my reminders organized?
DewCue separates reminders into Today, Upcoming, and Overdue. Today helps you see what needs attention now, Upcoming shows what is ahead, and Overdue helps you revisit unfinished reminders. The side panel shows the same reminder list.
See a simple work routine
What are buckets used for?
Buckets are a place for client or project update logs and notes. Use one to keep the context you will need when you return to a task. Bucket content stays local, and the Free plan includes three buckets.
Organize client follow-ups
Will reminders arrive when Chrome is closed or my computer is asleep?
Do not rely on an alert in those conditions. DewCue does not guarantee that every alarm will fire when Chrome, the computer, or the network is off. Test your actual setup, keep a separate backup for time-critical tasks, and review Today and Overdue when you return.
Test your notification setup

Email follow-ups

How do email follow-ups work in DewCue?
You choose an email to capture and set a time to return to it. DewCue can save its subject, sender name, and visible link after you start the capture. At the scheduled time, the reminder prompts you to open the conversation and check what needs to happen next.
Create a useful email follow-up
Can I use DewCue with Gmail or Outlook?
DewCue’s privacy policy documents on-screen email capture for Gmail, Outlook on the web, and Yahoo Mail. You start capture yourself, and site permission is required. DewCue does not ask you to sign in to your email account or give it mailbox access. Capture on other webmail services has not been verified.
Understand the email workflow
Does DewCue automatically detect whether someone has replied?
No. A scheduled follow-up asks you to check the conversation yourself. DewCue does not monitor your mailbox or automatically determine reply status. The Premium no-reply follow-up workflow helps you return to the email and decide whether another follow-up is needed.
Follow up without mailbox monitoring
Will DewCue send emails for me?
No. DewCue reminds you to revisit a conversation. You read the email, decide whether a reply is needed, and send any message yourself through your email service.
See what happens at follow-up time

Focus & wellbeing

Which timers are included?
DewCue includes a stopwatch, a countdown timer, and a Pomodoro option with a 25-minute focus session and a 5-minute break. Standard timers are included in Free. Premium adds custom timer lengths.
Plan a focus session
What can the wellbeing reminders help me remember?
Optional nudges can remind you about water, eyes, posture, and breaks. Choose the prompts that fit your day. The Free plan includes two wellness nudges, and Premium removes that Free plan limit.
Add a gentle break routine
Can I quiet the wellbeing nudges during meetings or after work?
Yes. Wellbeing nudges have quiet hours and a mute option so you can reduce interruptions during meetings or after work. Quiet-hour settings can sync through Chrome; the wellbeing nudge settings themselves stay on the computer you use.
Choose when nudges are useful

Birthdays & milestones

Can I keep birthdays and milestones in DewCue?
Yes. DewCue includes birthdays and milestones. Free allows five birthdays and one milestone; Premium removes those Free plan limits. These entries stay local to the computer you use. Check the available settings and saved details in your installed version before relying on them for an important date.
Understand birthdays and milestones
Do birthdays and milestones sync to another computer?
No. Birthdays and milestones are stored locally on the computer where you keep them. They are not included in the reminder data that uses Chrome sync. Check the original computer and preserve the dates you need before changing computers or uninstalling DewCue.
Plan for locally stored dates

Backup & devices

Can I download a backup of my DewCue data?
DewCue’s privacy policy describes a downloadable backup file that belongs to you and is not uploaded by DewCue. The published information does not specify the file format or exactly which records it includes. Confirm its contents before treating it as a complete copy of your data.
Understand backups and local data
Is Chrome sync a complete backup of DewCue?
No. DewCue uses Chrome sync for reminders, quiet hours, and the notification choice. Buckets, notes, birthdays, milestones, and other local data are outside that set. Check both the synced and local parts of your setup before changing computers.
Compare sync, local data, and backups
What should I check before moving DewCue to another computer?
Keep the original computer available, check that Chrome sync is enabled for the intended profile, and verify the reminders and synced settings on the new computer. Separately preserve the local information you need. Confirm the backup transfer process and your Premium device allowance before removing the old installation.
Follow the computer-change checklist
What happens to my data if I uninstall DewCue?
Uninstalling removes DewCue’s local copy. Chrome sync may still hold reminders on your Google account until Chrome removes them, but this is not a promise of recovery. Preserve important local information and confirm any backup recovery process before uninstalling. Removing Premium from a computer is a different action.
Check your data before uninstalling
Does restoring my Premium licence restore my reminder data?
No. DewCue’s licence server keeps the email address, licence status, and installation ID used for Premium. It does not keep reminders, email subjects, or page content. Restoring paid access and recovering your content are separate tasks.
Separate activation from data recovery
Can I restore a downloaded backup on a new computer?
The available product information does not document an import or restore procedure, or whether existing data would be merged or replaced. Ask hello@dewcue.com about your extension version and backup file before relying on it for a move. Keep the original data available until the transfer has been verified.
Prepare a verified transfer
Why are my notes and buckets missing on another computer?
Buckets, update logs, and local notes stay on the computer where you create them. Their absence on another computer does not by itself mean reminder sync has failed. Check the original computer for that content and review the separate sync and local-storage lists.
Find the right place for your data

Privacy

Does DewCue read or upload the pages I visit?
DewCue does not upload pages. Displaying an on-page reminder card does not read the page, and that option requests permission when enabled. A page title, URL, or highlighted text is saved only when you explicitly capture it through the toolbar or right-click action.
Understand deliberate page capture
What email information does DewCue save?
On supported webmail pages, DewCue can use the visible subject, sender name, and message link after you allow it on that site. It does not read the message body or inbox list, ask for email sign-in, or send email information to a DewCue server.
Review email capture details
What syncs, and what stays on my computer?
Reminders, quiet hours, and your notification choice can sync between computers using the same Chrome profile with sync enabled. Buckets, notes, timer data, wellbeing nudges, birthdays, milestones, themes, follow-up preferences, and completed history stay local. Premium licence records are stored separately on DewCue’s licence server.
Check sync, backups, and computer changes
Does DewCue sell my data?
DewCue states that it does not sell data or use analytics or advertising. Reminder content is separate from its Premium license records. The privacy overview explains what uses Chrome sync, what stays on your computer, and what is stored for Premium activation.
Read the privacy overview
What information is stored to activate Premium?
DewCue’s license server stores the email address you enter, your license status, and an installation ID for the activated computer. It does not store reminders, email subjects, or page content. The extension checks the license about once a day. Contact hello@dewcue.com if you have a question about a license record.
Read the Premium activation guide

Plans & billing

What is included in the Free plan?
Free includes five repeat reminders, three alarms, three buckets, standard timers, two wellness nudges, five birthdays, one milestone, and ten captured emails per month. The email capture allowance is monthly; the other numbers are the published Free plan limits.
See the complete plan comparison
What does Premium add?
Premium removes the published Free plan limits and adds custom timer lengths, extra themes, and the no-reply email follow-up workflow. You still check email replies yourself; Premium does not add mailbox monitoring or automatic email sending.
Compare Free and Premium
When does the 14-day Premium trial start?
The trial starts the first time you exceed a Free plan limit on that computer. It lasts 14 days and does not require a card. Free plan limits resume when the trial ends unless you upgrade.
Understand the trial and plans
How much does DewCue Premium cost?
The listed international prices are $4.99 per month or $39 per year. India pricing is ₹199 per month or ₹1,990 per year, plus 18% GST. International USD checkout is available only when enabled, so check the available checkout and final total before paying.
View monthly and yearly pricing
Is there a lifetime plan?
The launch offer lists lifetime access at $99 internationally, or ₹4,990 plus 18% GST in India, under a shared offer for the first 500 purchases. Lifetime covers the Premium features sold with it for as long as the extension is offered. Confirm availability at checkout; international USD checkout must be enabled.
Review the lifetime offer
Can I cancel my Premium subscription?
Yes. You can cancel a monthly or yearly subscription at any time. Cancellation stops the next charge, and Premium stays available until your paid period ends. It does not erase the reminders on your computer.
Read cancellation and refund details
Does DewCue offer a refund?
Request a full refund within seven days of your first payment, a Lifetime purchase, or a later renewal charge. Email hello@dewcue.com with the email address used at checkout. Refunds return through Razorpay to the original payment method and are processed within 5–7 working days; your bank may take longer to show the credit.
Read the refund policy
How are Premium payments handled?
Razorpay handles Premium checkout. Indian cards and UPI are charged in INR; international cards use USD only when that checkout is enabled. Razorpay handles card numbers and UPI IDs, and DewCue does not see them. Review your plan, tax, currency, and final amount before paying.
Choose a plan
Can Premium keep working if I lose internet access?
If the license check cannot connect, Premium continues for seven days from the last successful check. The extension normally rechecks about once a day. This license grace period is separate from reminder delivery and does not guarantee alerts when Chrome or your computer is off.
Understand Premium license checks
How do I get my Premium licence key or activation code?
A licence key is emailed to the address you provide. If you request a six-digit code, that code is emailed to you. Razorpay sends the payment receipt separately. Use the licence or requested code when activating Premium in DewCue, and keep the purchase email address available.
Prepare your Premium activation
I paid but cannot find my Premium activation email. What should I do?
Check the email address you supplied and look in spam or junk folders. A Razorpay receipt confirms the payment but is separate from the licence key or requested code. If the activation email is still missing, contact hello@dewcue.com with the email address used for the purchase and a brief description of the problem.
Resolve an activation email problem
Can I remove Premium from a computer without deleting my reminders?
Yes. Removing Premium from a computer inside DewCue unbinds that computer and does not delete its reminders. This is different from uninstalling the extension, which removes the local copy of your data.
Understand removing Premium from a computer
How do I use my existing Premium purchase on another computer?
Keep your purchase email and licence details available, and check Premium activation in DewCue on the new computer. Paid access uses an installation ID for each computer you activate. Confirm the computers included with your licence before adding another one; Chrome reminder sync does not establish Premium activation.
Prepare Premium for a computer change
