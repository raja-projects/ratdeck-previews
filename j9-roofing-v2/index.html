Know your policy

RCV or ACV? It matters.

From our guide, Understanding RCV vs ACV Home Insurance Policies. We'll read your policy with you before you file.

RCV

Replacement Cost Value

The amount needed to replace or repair your roof without taking off depreciation. More generous payouts, and less out of pocket after a storm.

ACV

Actual Cash Value

The cost to repair or replace, minus depreciation for wear and tear. Often standard in homeowners policies, and it can leave you paying more out of pocket.
