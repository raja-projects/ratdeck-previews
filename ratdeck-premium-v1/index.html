What is GEO and AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are the work of making your firm easy for AI assistants and AI search to find, understand and cite. In practice that means clear service pages, structured data, consistent listings and genuine reviews. The goal is for your firm to be named accurately when buyers ask AI who to hire.
