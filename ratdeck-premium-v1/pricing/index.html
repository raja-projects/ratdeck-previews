FAQ

Common questions.

How much does AI visibility cost?

Plans are $349 (Starter), $690 (Growth, recommended) or $1,190 (Pro) per month in USD. A one-time AI visibility audit is $149.

What is in the $149 audit?

10 real buyer questions tested in the leading AI assistants and Google with screenshots, a side-by-side with 3 competitors, a prioritized written fix list, 3 quick wins and a 20-minute walkthrough call, delivered in 5 business days. It is fully credited to your first month if you start a plan.

Is there a minimum term?

3 months, then month to month with 30 days' notice.

Is there a quarterly discount?

Yes, save 10% when you pay quarterly.

What if my AI visibility doesn't improve?

If it hasn't improved after 3 months, month 4 is free.

Which plan should I choose?

Most single-location firms start with Growth. Starter covers the foundations; Pro suits multiple locations.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody controls AI answers. We improve the information AI tools rely on and report honestly every month.
