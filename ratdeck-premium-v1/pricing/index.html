FAQ

Common questions.

How much does AI visibility cost?

RATdeck AI visibility plans are $349 (Starter), $690 (Growth, recommended) or $1,190 (Pro) per month in USD. A one-time AI visibility audit is $149.

Is there a minimum term?

Monthly plans have a 3-month minimum. After that they continue month to month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

Is there a discount for paying quarterly?

Yes. You save 10% when you pay quarterly.

What if my AI visibility doesn't improve?

If your AI visibility hasn't improved after 3 months, month 4 is free.

Which plan should I choose?

Most single-location firms start with Growth at $690 per month. Starter suits firms that want the foundations first; Pro suits multiple locations or service lines. Your free AI visibility check includes a recommendation.

Are there extra costs?

Any third-party costs, such as paid directory listings, a domain or hosting, are listed in your written proposal before anything is purchased.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody controls AI answers or search rankings. We improve the information AI tools rely on and report honestly every month.
