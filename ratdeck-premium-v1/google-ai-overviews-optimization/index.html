FAQ

Common questions.

What are Google AI Overviews?

AI-generated summaries at the top of some Google results, with links to the sources used.

How do I get my website cited in AI Overviews?

Be indexed and eligible to show a snippet in Google Search (Google publishes no ranking-position threshold), answer the question clearly on a focused page, and keep your business details accurate. Citation is never guaranteed.

Does Google Business Profile matter?

Yes, especially for local questions. We make sure it is complete and matches your website.

Is AI Mode the same as AI Overviews?

They are related Google features built on Google Search. We test both where available.

Will AI Overviews reduce my website traffic?

Some questions get answered without a click. That is why being named and cited, and turning those visits into inquiries, matters more.
