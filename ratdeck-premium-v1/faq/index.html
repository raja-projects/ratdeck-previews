FAQ

Common questions.

What does RATdeck do?

We help US firms get found, trusted and recommended by AI assistants and search engines through AI visibility (AEO, GEO, AI SEO), AI-ready websites, local SEO and reputation work.

How much does AI visibility cost?

Starter $349, Growth $690 (recommended) or $1,190 Pro per month in USD, 3-month minimum. A one-time AI visibility audit is $149.

Is there a discount?

Save 10% when you pay quarterly. If visibility hasn't improved after 3 months, month 4 is free.

Do you guarantee rankings, AI recommendations or leads?

No. Nobody controls search rankings or AI answers. We improve what those systems rely on and report honestly.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Once the agreed project balance is paid, the website and approved deliverables are yours. Third-party licences are identified separately.

Can you work with my current website?

Yes. We can audit, redesign, repair or extend an existing site when its platform and hosting remain suitable.

What happens after I contact you?

We review your website and priority, reply with useful observations and recommend the smallest sensible next step. If there is a fit, you get a written scope.

How do you communicate?

Phone +1 (737) 306-3068 and email info@ratdeck.com, Mon–Fri 9 AM–2:30 PM Eastern, with written updates.

Which AI tools do you test?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers, each reported separately.

Do you write fake reviews?

Never. Reviews come only from real clients in their own words.
