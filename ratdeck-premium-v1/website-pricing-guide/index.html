FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a small business website cost?

RATdeck starting prices: Quick Fix $390, 5-page site $690, 10-page site $1,190, custom from $1,990, all one-time in USD.

Are these the final prices?

They are starting prices for the scopes listed. Your written proposal confirms the final investment and any additional work.

Will the price change during the project?

Not for the agreed scope. Extra work is explained and approved first.

Are hosting and licences included?

The domain is separate and stays in your name. Hosting and third-party licences are identified separately.

Do you work with US businesses?

Yes. RATdeck works remotely with US businesses, with a US phone line.
