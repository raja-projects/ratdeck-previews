What AI tools look at for a medical clinic.

  1. 01

    Your website

    Specialty and condition pages, provider bios with credentials, location pages with hours and insurance, and FAQ content.

  2. 02

    Directories and profiles

    Zocdoc, Healthgrades, WebMD Care, Vitals and your Google Business Profile. AI tools often cite these, so accuracy and completeness matter.

  3. 03

    Reviews

    Genuine, recent reviews that mention specific services.

  4. 04

    Third-party mentions

    Local press, associations and respected industry sites.

  5. 05

    Consistency

    The same name, address, phone and services everywhere.
