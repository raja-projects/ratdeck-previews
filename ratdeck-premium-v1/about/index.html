Our story

From websites to AI visibility.

RATdeck started in 2017, when Anisha founded the business to plan and build websites for service businesses. Since then the team has delivered more than 1,000 projects across web design, WordPress development, SEO and the creative work around them.

As more clients began asking AI assistants who to hire, the work grew with them. Today RATdeck helps US law firms, dental practices, accountants and B2B companies become easier for AI tools and search engines to understand, trust and recommend, through AI visibility (AEO and GEO), reputation management and AI-ready websites.

RATdeck, a brand of Tanishkha. We are a small remote team working US business hours, and you work directly with the people responsible for planning and delivery.
