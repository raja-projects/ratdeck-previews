What it means

AI visibility, AEO and GEO.

AI visibility is how clearly and accurately AI assistants can find, describe and recommend your business. The work is also called Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) or AI SEO. The labels differ; the goal is the same.

  1. 01

    Technical access

    We check that AI crawlers can read your website, then add accurate structured data (Organization, your professional type, Service, FAQ) and an llms.txt file.

  2. 02

    Consistent information everywhere

    We align your name, services, locations, hours and descriptions across Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and your industry's directories.

  3. 03

    Answer-ready content

    We publish clear pages that directly answer the questions your clients ask, written in your voice and approved by you.

  4. 04

    Genuine trust signals

    AI citation and mention building: we help you earn real reviews and third-party mentions. Never fake reviews or undisclosed promotion.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is AI visibility?

How clearly and accurately AI assistants can find, describe and recommend your firm.

What is the difference between AEO, GEO and AI SEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) focuses on pages that answer buyer questions directly. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) focuses on the outside sources AI tools draw on, such as listings, reviews and mentions. AI SEO combines both with classic search work. We do all three in one plan.

How long does it take?

Technical fixes are usually picked up within weeks. Changes in AI answers usually take one to three months and vary by tool and market.

Is this different from SEO?

It builds on SEO. AI tools also weigh directories, reviews, third-party mentions and how consistently your firm is described.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. We improve what you have. AI-ready websites from $390 are available if you need one.

Do you guarantee AI assistants will recommend us?

No. Nobody controls AI answers. We improve the information those tools rely on and report honestly every month.
